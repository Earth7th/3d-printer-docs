# Drying Filament (Wet Filament Fix)

Most 3D-printing filaments are **hygroscopic** — they absorb moisture from the air. Wet
filament prints badly no matter how well you've calibrated, so drying is a prerequisite
for good prints and for trustworthy [calibration](../calibration/filament-calibration.md)
(especially retraction, stringing, and bridging).

## How to tell filament is wet

- **Pockmarks / tiny dots / bubbles** on the extruded strands (steam escaping).
- **Popping, crackling, or faint steam** at the nozzle while printing.
- **Excessive stringing** that retraction tuning can't fully fix.
- **Rough, fuzzy, or dull surfaces**; inconsistent extrusion.
- **Weak, brittle layers** that snap apart easily.

## Drying temperatures and times

Dry **below** the material's softening point. Times assume a filament dryer or dehydrator;
badly soaked spools need longer.

| Material | Temp | Time |
|----------|------|------|
| PLA | 45–55 °C | 4–6 h |
| **PETG** | **60–65 °C** | **6–8 h** |
| ABS / ASA | 65–80 °C | 4–6 h |
| TPU | 45–55 °C | 6–8 h |
| Nylon (PA) | 70–80 °C | 8–12 h |
| PC (Polycarbonate) | 70–80 °C | 6–8 h |

## How to dry

- **Filament dryer** (best) — purpose-built, correct low temps, often lets you print
  straight from it.
- **Food dehydrator** — works well; check it holds a low, even temperature.
- **Oven** — ⚠️ risky. Many home ovens run hot and uneven and can soften/melt a spool.
  Only if you must: stay well below the softening point, verify with a separate
  thermometer, and don't leave it unattended.

## Keep it dry after

Drying is temporary if the spool goes back into open air.

- Store in a **sealed box or vacuum bag with fresh desiccant** (silica gel).
- Print from a **dry box** for thirsty materials (PETG, Nylon, TPU, PC).
- Recharge/replace desiccant when it's saturated.

## Creality K2 / CFS note

The CFS multi-material unit is an enclosed box but is **not an active heated dryer** — add
**desiccant packs inside it** and keep unused spools sealed. Dry a wet spool separately
before loading it.
